Research subagent report, kept verbatim as the evidence behind `docs/business-plan.md` and the prices in
`src/data/site.ts`. Method: WebFetch of each pricing page plus WebSearch sweeps. **[UNVERIFIED]** =
secondhand or snippet-only; **[STALE]** = source older than 2025. Thin slices: solo Squarespace/Wix
designers in Lancaster, and York/Harrisburg freelancers (search budget ran out).

Pros: predictable MRR, easier close for cash-tight owners, longer client lifetime. Cons: you finance
the build (a $3,500 build at $150/mo breaks even around month 20); edit workload spikes; the model has
a "hostage" reputation that WebTek exploits locally. A 12-month minimum with ownership transfer at
month 12 (Redefine's structure) is the cleanest version.

## (f) Payment and contract norms

- Deposits: under $2,000 → 50/50; $2,000–$10,000 → 40–50% deposit then milestones; over $10,000 →
  25–35% plus milestones (https://reallygooddesigns.com/upfront-payment-charge/). Local: thirds (RedX),
  50/50 (Noble).
- Revisions: two rounds per milestone is the template default; extra rounds hourly or ~$150/round.
  Feedback window 2–3 business days; pause after 10–14 days of client inactivity.
- Timelines: template 5–7 pages 2–4 weeks; custom 10–15 pages 4–8 weeks. Local: Keystone 3–5 weeks,
  Noble 3–5 / 5–8, Redefine 3–4, WebTek 8–10, WebFX 3–12 months. Every source blames client content.
- Ownership: copyright transfers on final payment; domain in the client's name; files handed over;
  hosting transfer spelled out (https://rynoss.com/blog/who-owns-my-website/ ; *Conwell v. Gray Loon*
  turned on the absence of a written assignment:
  https://jolt.law.harvard.edu/digest/conwell-v-gray-loon-outdoor-marketing-group-inc).

## (g) Synthesis

The gap between $3,500 and $6,500 for a 5-page site is where a local owner reads "real business, not a
side hustle, but not a Harrisburg agency." Below $2,500 is Keystone's lane; above $7,000 needs an
agency's visible team.

| Offer | Band | Why |
|---|---|---|
| One-pager | $1,200–$2,000 | above Burk's $55/mo + $1,500 and Webflow freelancers' floor |
| 5-page brochure | $3,500–$5,500 | with Rachel Kline/RedX, above Seva/Burk, under WebFX |
| 10–20 pages | $6,000–$10,000 | WebTek's "many projects" band |
| Small e-commerce | $7,500–$12,000 | Burk $5,500 + $165/mo, RedX $6k + $125/mo |
| Care plans | $75 / $175–$200 / $350 | between Keystone/Seva's $50–$55 and Rachel Kline's $259 |
| Local SEO | $600–$1,200/mo | Ahrefs freelancer avg $1,150, Arc4 $500–$1,200, Burk $900, Keystone $600 |
| GBP-only | $300–$450/mo | above the "$200 = automated" line |
| GEO | inside the SEO retainer | SE Ranking's $937 is agency; Reach Right bundles at $297 |
| Subscription option | $175–$250/mo, 12-month minimum, ownership at month 12 | Redefine's structure; keep the upfront option as default |

Positioning notes: speed is a real differentiator (nobody local promises under 3 weeks); publish prices
precisely; Burk owns the "Kingdom centric" phrasing in Lititz, so lead with craft, ownership from day
one and results, and let values show in how the work is done.
