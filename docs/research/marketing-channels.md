# Getting the first 10–20 clients: a Lititz, PA web-design + SEO shop, 2026–2027 (researched 2026-10-27)

Research subagent report, kept as the evidence behind the marketing section of `docs/business-plan.md`.
Every number carries its URL; **[unverified]** = not confirmed from a primary source. Follow-ups
flagged in §8 (BNI chapter list, Lititz Rotary dues, Northern Lancaster County Chamber dues, exact
Keyword Planner volumes).

## 1. Headline conclusions

1. **Paid search is viable but not cheap at $300/mo.** WordStream/LocaliQ 2026 (13,000+ campaigns): Business Services **$5.87 CPC, 4.85% CVR, $93.69 CPL** (https://localiq.com/blog/search-advertising-benchmarks/). Web-design keywords run higher: "web design services" ~$19.86 CPC, "website redesign" ~$23.39 (https://seojuice.io/keyword-research/digital-marketing/digital-design) **[third-party]**. Working estimate: local long-tail CPC $6–$15, landing-page CVR 5–8%, **$75–$300 per raw lead**, roughly half qualified. **3–6 qualified leads/month needs ~$800–$1,500/mo; $300/mo buys 1–3 raw leads.**
2. **Google Local Services Ads is closed to this business** — no marketing/web design/SEO category (https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/6224841).
3. **The cheapest first-10 path is relational:** Lancaster Chamber ($550/yr), free SCORE/ASSETS/library ecosystems, referral partners at 10%, personalized Loom site audits (10–16% reply vs ~4% for plain cold email). Paid media accelerates clients 10–20.
4. **Compliance surprise:** Pennsylvania SB 992 took effect **October 18, 2026**: texts and ringless voicemail are "telephone solicitation," prior-express-written-consent standard, solicitation hours 9 AM–7 PM, never Sundays (https://www.kelleydrye.com/viewpoints/blogs/ad-law-access/state-telemarketing-update-what-to-know-about-changes-coming-in-pennsylvania). Cold SMS is out; cold email is in (CAN-SPAM, opt-out, no B2B exception).
5. **Sales tax is the open question for a CPA before the first invoice:** per CPA summaries of the DOR's late-2024 guidance, "website development, if the site is transferred to the customer" is taxable, hosting nontaxable (https://www.wipfli.com/insights/articles/tax-updated-tax-guidance-pa-software-and-digital-goods). Primary DOR pages returned 403; a PA web firm's table lists web development as non-taxable citing 2020/2022 DOR answers (https://aurisecreative.com/sales-tax/). **Sources conflict.**

## 3. Channel detail (condensed)

- **Google Ads:** exact/phrase only, Lititz + Lancaster + Ephrata + Manheim geo, one landing page with a "see your site's score" form, negatives for "jobs," "course," "free," "template." Competitors ranking organically for "web design lancaster pa": WebFX, EZMarketing, Rise, RedX, lancasterpa.com directory — expect WebFX-scale bidders on broad terms.
- **GBP:** eligible as a service-area business that travels to customers (do discovery meetings at client locations); hide the address; "Website designer" primary, "Internet marketing service" / "Marketing agency" secondary [confirm labels]; ask every client for a review at launch and at 60 days; post weekly (before/after, PageSpeed scores).
- **Meta:** detailed-targeting exclusions removed Jan 2025, further consolidation Jan 2026; plan on creative-as-targeting plus retargeting site visitors; before/after with a measurable result, sound-off mobile video. Realistic: $5–$10/day retargeting plus one lead-form campaign per month.
- **Referral partners:** photographers, printers/sign shops, bookkeepers/CPAs, business attorneys, insurance agents, Chamber staff, SCORE mentors, marketing freelancers who do not build, IT MSPs. Flat **10% of the first project invoice** within 7 days of payment, plus reciprocal referrals.
- **Cold outreach:** 90-second screen recording of their site on mobile, PageSpeed score, two concrete fixes, "I sketched a new homepage, want to see it?" Ten per week → ~4–6 replies and 1–2 proposals a month. CAN-SPAM: accurate headers, postal address, opt-out within 10 business days, up to $53,088 per email (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business). **No cold texting** (TCPA + SB 992); phone follow-up business hours only, never Sundays.
- **Content/inbound:** long-tail openings "website cost lancaster pa," "[town] small business website," "church website lancaster," per-industry pages. A free "site check" page converts weakly on its own but doubles as the Ads landing page and the Loom hook; require name + business + phone, follow up within 24 h. Case-study pages with numbers are the strongest content; aim for three before spending on ads.

## 5. Funnel benchmarks

Visitor → inquiry: B2B median 2.23%, professional services ~4.6%; plan 2–3% for a new site. Inquiry →
proposal: 40–50%. Proposal → close: Proposify 2026 average 34%, digital marketing 48%; plan 35–45%.
Sales cycle: SMB B2B under $15k 14–42 days; plan 3–6 weeks, churches/nonprofits 2–3× longer. **Implied:
10 clients ≈ 25 proposals ≈ 55 qualified conversations ≈ 110 raw inquiries, far fewer when referrals
(50%+ close) carry the load.**

## 6. 90-day launch sequence

**Days 1–14, foundation (10–15 h):** fictitious name under the LLC; CPA call on sales tax; bind GL +
E&O; adopt the contract; shop site live with a "site check" page and case studies; GBP as an SAB with
hidden address and service areas Lititz, Lancaster, Ephrata, Manheim, Lebanon, York, Harrisburg,
Hershey, Reading; claim free Nextdoor, Alignable, Clutch, DesignRush, UpCity profiles; CAN-SPAM-
compliant email tool with the postal address.

**Days 15–45, relationships (4–6 h/wk):** join Lancaster Chamber; first Monthly Mixer and one Get
Connected; a Northern Lancaster County "Chamber Chat"; ASSETS events; request a SCORE mentor; ten
referral-partner coffees with a written 10% offer; Loom audits at ten per week aimed at
Lititz/Ephrata/Manheim businesses with slow or non-mobile sites; ask the first two clients for reviews.

**Days 46–90, paid test (3–4 h/wk):** Google Search at $300–$500/mo on exact/phrase local terms to the
site-check page, measure CPL for 45 days; Meta retargeting at $5/day with a before/after video; two
local articles; weekly GBP and LinkedIn posts; first speaking slot (library, SCORE, Manheim chamber).
**Day-90 target:** 4–6 signed clients, 2 referral partners who have sent a lead, GBP with 5+ reviews.
